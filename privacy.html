Privacy

Pink Accounting & Tax Solutions Pty Ltd (ABN 51 682 301 891) handles personal information under the Privacy Act 1988. Job Profit is a service of this firm. This page applies to the whole practice.

What we collect

Name, contact details, business details, and the financial and tax information needed to provide accounting and tax services. If you book a call or email us, we keep that correspondence.

Why we collect it

To provide the service you asked for, meet our tax-agent and legal obligations, and run the practice. We do not sell lists.

Who we share it with

Only where the job requires it: the ATO, ASIC, your bank or software provider with your authority, professional indemnity insurers, and regulators when the law requires it. Business-grade tools used inside the firm are bound by our confidentiality duties.

How to contact us

Privacy questions and access requests: admin@pinktax.com.au or 07 3544 6386. Shop 15A, 18–22 Kremzow Rd, Brendale QLD 4500.

If we cannot resolve a privacy complaint, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.
