How Pink started

Pink Accounting was founded in 2020 by Huong (Pinky) Bui, helping South-East Queensland business owners through the hardest trading many of them had seen. The practice incorporated as Pink Accounting & Tax Solutions Pty Ltd in November 2024, which is why the current ABN shows a 2024 start date. The work began in 2020.

Huong holds a Master of Professional Accounting (Griffith), is MIPA AFA, and is Registered Tax Agent 26284368, with more than ten years in financial accounting. You deal with the principal. The firm stays small on purpose.
