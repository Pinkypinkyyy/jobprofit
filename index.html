The problem

Tax is the end result of decisions made months earlier.

If the first close look at a transaction is when the return is prepared, it can still be reported correctly. The chance to make a better decision has often already passed. For a job-based business that gap shows up in quotes, plant, contractors and cash.

01

The return is last
