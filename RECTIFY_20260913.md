# Service Profit website rectify — 13 September 2026

Source: full-site audit dated "checked live 14 Sep 2026", 10 findings.
Repo: Pinkypinkyyy/jobprofit · Live origin: https://www.serviceprofit.com.au/

The live origin is not reachable from this session, so every finding was
checked against the generators that produce the site, not against the
served HTML. Two findings did not survive that check. They are recorded
below with the evidence rather than actioned.

## Red team on the audit

| # | Audit said | What the source shows | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `hvac.html`, `electrical.html`, `construction.html` serve "a byte-for-byte copy of the homepage (identical checksum)". Restore the three pages. | Each file is a 463-469 byte redirect stub built by `redirect_home()` in `tools/build_pages.py`. `index.html` is 19.5 KB. The checksums are not identical and never were. The collapse was deliberate: `docs/REDTEAM_ONE_OFFER.md` records the three pages as "fake products", and two tests already lock the behaviour in — `test_old_trade_urls_redirect_home` and `test_sitemap_has_real_pages_not_fake_trades`. Their absence from the sitemap is correct; redirects do not belong in a sitemap. | **Rejected.** Restoring the pages would reverse a decision taken on 12 September and put thin duplicate content back on the site. A 301 is not available: GitHub Pages serves no redirect rules. Canonical to "/" plus meta refresh plus `location.replace` is the strongest consolidation signal this host allows, and it is already in place. Added a viewport tag so the fallback line renders properly on a phone. |
| 4 | The TPB register showing "Professional associations: None on record" means the site and the register "disagree about the one fact the disclaimer depends on". | The TPB professional-association field is self-reported and optional. It is not what makes a Professional Standards scheme apply. Scheme cover turns on IPA membership plus a current Professional Practice Certificate. An empty TPB field is not evidence of non-participation. | **Not actioned in code.** The line stays up. The action is Huong's and unchanged: get written confirmation from IPA that public-practice status is covered by their scheme, and add IPA to the TPB profile. If IPA confirms no cover, the line comes off `footer()` in `tools/shared.py`, which is the single place it is written. |

## Not fixed, and why

- **7, captions.** No transcript of the film exists anywhere in the repo and the
  audio was not transcribable in this session. Caption text for a client-facing
  film is not something to invent. Supply the script and the burn-in plus a
  WebVTT track is a small job.
- **8, booking friction and the Friday mismatch.** Microsoft Bookings has
  Fridays closed; `contact.html` says Mon-Fri 9:00-4:30. Both sides of that are
  Bookings admin settings inside the Microsoft product, not repository content.
  Decide which is right and set it there. The site copy is correct if Friday
  trading is correct.
- **9, first-party endpoint.** GitHub Pages serves static files only. Moving off
  `formsubmit.co` needs somewhere to post to. Captcha is on in the meantime.
