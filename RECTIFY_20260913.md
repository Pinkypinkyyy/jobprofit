## Not fixed, and why

- **7, captions.** No transcript of the film exists anywhere in the repo and the
  audio was not transcribable in this session. Caption text for a client-facing
  film is not something to invent. Supply the script and the burn-in plus a
  WebVTT track is a small job.
- **8, booking friction and the Friday mismatch.** Microsoft Bookings has
  Fridays closed; `contact.html` says Mon-Fri 9:00-4:30. Both sides of that are
  Bookings admin settings inside the Microsoft product, not repository content.
  Decide which is right and set it there. The site copy is correct if Friday
  trading is correct.
- **9, first-party endpoint.** GitHub Pages serves static files only. Moving off
  `formsubmit.co` needs somewhere to post to. Captcha is on in the meantime.

# Whole-site review — same day

The audit above covered ten specific items. This is the broader pass over
message, copy, tone, conversion and SEO that the audit did not do.

## Changed

| Area | Finding | Change |
|---|---|---|
| Conversion | `book.html` asked for **ten required fields including three required essay boxes** before a tradie could reach the calendar. This is the only conversion page on the site and the heaviest ask sits at the top of it. | "What is hurting" stays required, because it is what makes the call useful. "Where is the business now" and "where do you want it in 12 months" are now optional and marked as optional. Eight required fields, one essay box. |
| SEO | Homepage title was 98 characters, so it truncated in results, and it led with the brand rather than the thing buyers search for. | Now "Air con, electrical and construction accountant \| Brisbane" (58). Brand moved to a new `og:site_name`. |
| SEO | `system.html`, `why.html` and `check.html` had 26-28 character titles carrying no service or location term at all. `pricing.html` and `rights.html` ran long. | All six rewritten to fit a result and carry the term someone would actually type. A test now fails any title over 62 characters. |
| SEO | `sameAs` listed Facebook and LinkedIn but **not the Google Business Profile**, even though `RECTIFY_20260911` records it as delivered. It is the strongest entity signal a local firm has, and it was the one missing. | Added to `business_node()`, which `local_business_node()` derives from, so both the homepage and contact schema carry it. |
| Tone | Em dashes in our own copy: the `system.html` lead and four lines of the `rights.html` register list. House style forbids them. | Rewritten as full stops. The one Google review quote keeps its em dash because it is verbatim. A test enforces this. |

## Checked and left alone

- **Internal linking is sound.** Every real page has ten inbound internal links;
  `check.html` is not orphaned, it sits in the footer. The only pages with no
  inbound links are `404.html` and the three redirect stubs, which is correct.
- **No `aggregateRating` schema, and that is right.** The 5.0 and the review
  count come from Google. Marking up third-party reviews as your own
  aggregateRating breaches Google's structured data policy. Leave it off.
- **Claims discipline holds across every page.** Worked examples are labelled as
  worked examples, exclusions are stated next to inclusions, "not unlimited
  work" and "you have not signed anything" appear where the fee does.
- **Logo alt text stays `alt="pink"`.** It looked like an accessibility defect,
  but `docs/PINK_BRAND_ON_SERVICE_PROFIT.md` locks the lowercase pink wordmark
  and the nav logo already sits inside a link carrying
  `aria-label="Pink Accounting, Service Profit"`. Brand lock wins.
- **Fee architecture is coherent.** $550 Compliance, $1,650 Job Profit,
  $2,650 Weekly Visibility, $3,500 Ready to Scale, bookkeeping as an add-on and
  not a plan. Cumulative inclusions are stated. The pricing title deliberately
  does not lead with "from $550", which would anchor buyers on the cheapest
  tier when most files are $1,650.

## Still open, and not code

- **No CI runs on pull requests.** The only workflow deploys Pages on push to
  `main`. `tests/test_site.py` never runs in CI on any branch, so these thirty
  tests only protect the site if someone runs them locally.
- **The homepage carries two competing calls to action** ("Book 15 minutes" and
  "Free 60-second hours check"). Both are reasonable. Which one earns the hero
  is a question for GA4 once there is enough traffic to answer it, not a
  question to settle by opinion now.
